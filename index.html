Terms and Conditions

Please read this before you use the app.

  • Estimates only. The app shows estimates calculated from the figures you enter. It is not your lender's statement, and your lender's records prevail.
  • No financial advice. The app does not tell you to borrow, repay or stop borrowing.
  • Your data. The app stores your email address and only the loan amounts, fees, dates and lender types that you enter. It does not read your SMS messages or contacts.
  • Where it is stored. Your data is stored online with Supabase, and the servers may be outside Kenya. Other app users cannot see your records. The project owner can see the database for testing and support.
  • Your rights. Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you can delete your account and all your data at any time with the Delete my account button.
  • Internet needed. The app needs an internet connection to save and load data.
  • University project. This app is part of a student project at Riara University. Results from the test with classmates are early evidence and do not apply to all students.
  • No lender link. The app does not connect to Fuliza, M-Shwari or any other lender and cannot make payments.
